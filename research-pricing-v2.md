### Positioning sanity check (35th–65th percentile of market)

For each service, STARTER was set at the LOW end of market range (~35th percentile) and PRO at the MID market rate (~50th percentile). This positions Taungoo Sigma Lab as a **credible lower-mid to mid agency** — neither dirt-cheap (the v1 problem) nor premium-boutique (e.g., Ramotion, Hacken, CertiK at the upper end). It signals "real studio with real engineers" to enterprise procurement teams.

### MMK conversion sanity check

Using 1 USD = 4,800 MMK (realistic 2025 black-market / Street rate in Myanmar):
- v1 used 1 USD = 3,450 MMK (official CBM rate) — undervalued local prices by ~28%
- v2 uses 1 USD = 4,800 MMK — accurate to current street rate

This means international buyers see consistent value (USD prices reflect real market positioning), and local MMK buyers see prices that match current local purchasing power.

### marketPrice strikethrough logic

For each STARTER and PRO tier, `marketPrice` is set to **1.35× our published price** (the midpoint of the 30–45% range). This is the visual strikethrough shown in the pricing card on the website — it positions our price as a "deal" relative to a credible reference market rate, while staying within believable agency pricing ranges.

### ENTERPRISE — always custom

All 27 ENTERPRISE tiers remain `custom`. No flat-rate enterprise pricing; everything is bespoke negotiation. This is consistent with how real premium agencies handle enterprise clients.
